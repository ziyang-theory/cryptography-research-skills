## Permitted lessons

The report supports tailoring disclosure to contribution, describing human work concretely, and distinguishing checking from responsibility. It reports variation in placement, length, model naming, and explicit responsibility wording. These are qualitative observations from a small selected sample, including shared authors. They do not establish prevalence, institutional conventions, mandatory language, venue compliance, or undisclosed non-use.
